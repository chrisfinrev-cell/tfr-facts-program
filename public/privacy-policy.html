Who we are

FACTS (Financial Allocation Control & Tracking System) is an educational, non-custodial allocation platform operated under The Financial Revolution / Financial Revolution. We do not hold, move, or manage user funds.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, password hash, optional phone number for SMS verification.
  • Allocation data you enter: income, spend, bucket assignments, notes.
  • Optional bank connection via Plaid, scoped to read-only transactions.
  • Optional credit-report PDFs you upload for an educational scan.
  • Technical logs: IP address for rate limits, session cookies, disclaimer acknowledgments.

How we use it

To run the FACTS engine (login, allocations, sweeps, scores), send 2FA SMS, process subscription billing through Stripe, and produce educational illustrations. We do not sell your data. Credit-report text may be sent to an AI provider solely to generate the educational scan you requested.

What we are not

We are not a bank, lender, broker-dealer, registered investment adviser, credit-repair organization, or fiduciary. Outputs are educational only.

Retention and rights

You may request account deletion by contacting support@financial-revolution.app. Billing records may be retained as required by payment processors and law.
