Model how parking income in a HELOC reduces total interest paid
Calc 1
How This Works
Instead of letting your paycheck sit in a 0% checking account, you deposit it directly into your HELOC — temporarily reducing the balance. Your expenses draw it back throughout the month. The net effect: a lower average daily balance means less interest accrues on your HELOC. Over time, your monthly surplus chips away at your primary mortgage principal.

📚 Understanding HELOC Velocity
✅ When It Works
  • Positive monthly cash flow (income exceeds expenses)
  • HELOC rate is lower than the interest your savings earn
  • Disciplined spending — no lifestyle creep
  • Low-variable-rate environment
  • Primary mortgage has no prepayment penalties
⚠️ When It Doesn't
  • Negative or tight cash flow — the strategy needs surplus
  • Rising rate environment erodes HELOC advantage
  • Home value drops, reducing available equity
  • Spending habits change once HELOC is open
  • Bank freezes or reduces HELOC limit
⚡ Key Risks
  • HELOC rates are variable — can spike significantly
  • Your home is collateral; missed payments risk foreclosure
  • Behavioral risk: treating HELOC as emergency spending fund
  • Complex to track vs. simple extra principal payments
  • Not a magic shortcut — it's a cash-flow optimization tool
The Bottom Line: HELOC velocity is a legitimate cash-flow optimization strategy used by financially disciplined homeowners. The math works when executed correctly. But it requires consistent positive cash flow, rate awareness, and behavioral discipline. The calculators above show the potential — your advisor helps you execute safely.
